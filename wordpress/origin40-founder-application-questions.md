# Origin40 Founder Application Questions - Final Lean Version

Use this as the review copy for the final Fluent Forms JSON import file:
`wordpress/origin40-fluentform-founder-application-v5-final.json`.

Current upload recommendation: use `v5-final`. It is shorter, early-founder-friendly, and includes
the 100% online live-session requirement.

Styling file: `wordpress/origin40-fluentform-style.css`.

## Application Intent

This form is designed for early founders, including people with only an idea. It does not require
traction, revenue, registration, or a live product. It screens for clarity, seriousness, build
readiness, coachability, and ability to attend the 4-week Origin40 build sprint.

## Section 1 - Eligibility and Contact

1. Full name
2. Email address
3. WhatsApp number
4. Country of residence
5. City / state
6. LinkedIn, portfolio, GitHub, or public profile
7. Are you applying as a solo founder or as a team?

## Section 2 - Idea or Startup Snapshot

8. Startup, business, or project name
9. One-line pitch
10. Current stage
11. Sector / industry
12. What are you building or hoping to build?
13. Who is it for?
14. What problem are you solving, and why does it matter?

## Section 3 - Early Validation

15. What makes you believe this problem is real?
16. How many potential users/customers have you spoken to?
17. What do you have so far? Select all that apply.
18. Optional link: demo, deck, prototype, landing page, or notes

## Section 4 - Build Sprint Readiness

19. What exists today?
20. What can you realistically build or validate in 4 weeks?
21. What would you like to show on Demo Day?
22. Who will build or drive the build?
23. Do you have a laptop and reliable internet for build sessions?

## Section 5 - Business Basics

24. How might this become a business?
25. What alternatives do people use today?
26. Revenue status
27. Is the business registered?
28. Any legal, data, IP, finance, or compliance concern we should know about?

## Section 6 - Commitment and Program Fit

29. Can you attend live online build sessions every Monday and Friday for 4 weeks?
30. Can you commit at least 10-15 hours weekly outside sessions?
31. Can you use Discord as the official Origin40 communication hub?
32. Which mentor areas do you need most? Select up to 3.
33. What is your biggest blocker right now?
34. Why do you want to join Origin40 now?

## Section 7 - Consent and Submission

35. Data and application review consent
36. Accuracy confirmation
37. Media/testimonial consent

## Hidden / Admin Fields

- `source`
- `utm_source`
- `utm_medium`
- `utm_campaign`
- `application_status`

## Recommended Review Rubric

| Area | Weight | What to look for |
|---|---:|---|
| Founder seriousness | 20 | Commitment, responsiveness, clarity, willingness to build |
| Problem clarity | 20 | Specific customer, real pain, thoughtful insight |
| Build readiness | 20 | Realistic 4-week milestone, laptop/internet, builder ownership |
| Market and business | 15 | Coherent customer, alternatives, early business hypothesis |
| Validation | 15 | Conversations, lived experience, evidence, early signal |
| Program fit | 10 | online live-session availability, Discord readiness, coachability |
