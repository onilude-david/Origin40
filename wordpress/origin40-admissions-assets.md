This is the working manifest for the public application form and related WordPress assets.

| Asset | File | Use |

## Positioning

The final form is intentionally lean and early-founder friendly. It accepts applicants who are:

- idea-only founders
- doing customer discovery
- building a prototype
- working on an MVP
- live with users
- already testing revenue or pilots

## Must-Know Requirement

The application clearly states that Origin40 is **100% online** and that live build sessions happen every Monday
and Friday for 4 weeks.

## Review Focus

Use the form to screen for:

- founder seriousness
- problem clarity
- build readiness
- early validation
- business thinking
- legal/finance awareness
- Discord readiness
- Online Session Availability
