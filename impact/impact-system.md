# Origin40 — Impact, Funding & Follow-Up System

Data: tabs `19 · Follow-Up Tracker`, `19b · Alumni Tracker`, `19c · Impact Dashboard`.

---

## 1. Impact Dashboard (tab 19c)

Auto-rolls the headline numbers funders care about:
- Founders started → completed → **completion rate**
- **MVPs live** at Day 90
- Total users reached · total alumni revenue · total funding raised
- Demo Day Priority founders (warm-intro ready)

Use these numbers in the impact report, partner thank-yous, and the next cohort's marketing.

---

## 2. 30/60/90-Day Follow-Up (tab 19)

One row per founder per stage (Day 30 / 60 / 90). Capture: MVP live? · users/customers · revenue ·
funding raised · **Funder Readiness** · notes.

**Cadence & ownership:** schedule the three check-ins at completion; an owner runs each (15-min call or
form). Don't let alumni go dark — this is where outcomes (and funder stories) actually happen.

---

## 3. Funder Readiness (statuses)

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Not Ready** | Early; no real traction yet | Keep building; mentor support |
| **Almost Ready** | Some traction; gaps remain | Targeted help (deck/metrics) |
| **Ready for Warm Introduction** | Credible traction + clear ask | Make intros to relevant funders |
| **Demo Day Priority** | Strongest; investor-ready now | Fast-track to investor pack + intros |

**Funder Readiness Checklist (per founder):**
- [ ] Clear problem + evidence it's real
- [ ] Working product (live)
- [ ] Real users + a traction metric trending up
- [ ] Credible business model + unit economics
- [ ] A specific, proportionate ask
- [ ] A tight one-pager + 3-min pitch
- [ ] Founder can answer the 8 hardest questions

---

## 4. Alumni Tracker (tab 19b)

Long-term outcomes: still building? · current stage · sector · opportunities · warm intros made? ·
outcome. This is your evidence base and your network — keep it alive beyond 90 days.

---

## 5. Opportunity Pipeline

Match founders to opportunities (investors, grants, accelerators, pilots, jobs, partners). Track in
the Alumni tab "Opportunity" + "Warm Intro Made?" columns, or extend tab 14 logic. Source opportunities
from Opportunity Partners (see `partners/sponsor-system.md`).

---

## 6. Impact Report (structure)

1. **Cover** — Origin40, cohort, dates, the legacy line.
2. **Executive summary** — the headline numbers (from tab 19c).
3. **The program** — what we did over 4 weeks.
4. **The founders** — selected stats; a few featured stories (problem → product → traction).
5. **Outcomes** — MVPs shipped, users, revenue, funding, warm intros.
6. **Demo Day** — highlights, winners, attendance, investors present.
7. **Mentors & partners** — who made it possible (thank-yous, logos).
8. **What's next** — alumni support + the next cohort.
9. **Appendix** — methodology + full metrics.

---

## 7. Storytelling / Documentation Plan

- **Capture continuously:** weekly founder clips, build screenshots, quotes, Demo Day footage.
- **Founder story arc:** "before" (idea/struggle) → "build" (the 4 weeks) → "after" (traction/outcome).
- **Formats:** carousels, short video, written case studies, the impact report, press features.
- **Cadence:** Phase 8 weekly updates during the program; Phase 10 storytelling after (see `marketing/`).
- **Consent:** ensure media consent is on file (see `program-management/legal-consent-disclaimers.md`)
  before publishing any founder's name, image, or story.
