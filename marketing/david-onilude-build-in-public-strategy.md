# David Onilude — Build Origin40 in Public

**Campaign window:** July 9–August 31, 2026  
**Core series:** *Building Origin40*  
**Narrative:** Can a small team help 19 African founders move from ideas to credible products in
three online build weeks and one Lagos showcase?

## Strategic choice

This is a personal operator documentary, not a second Origin40 corporate account.

- **Origin40 account:** official announcements, schedules, founder recognition, partner visibility,
  and programme results.
- **David's account:** decisions, doubts, lessons, behind-the-scenes work, personal reflections, and
  conversations with the people doing the work.

The programme is the plot. David is the guide. The founders are the protagonists.

## The five content pillars

### 1. The Operator's Diary

What it takes to build and run Origin40: admissions, programme design, difficult calls, deadlines,
team coordination, and the moments nobody sees.

### 2. From Idea to Product

Founder progress, customer evidence, MVP decisions, before/after moments, and weekly milestone
stories—with consent and founders presented as capable people, not rescue stories.

### 3. Lessons From the Build

Short, useful lessons drawn from work completed that week: selecting founders, recruiting mentors,
scoping MVPs, running online programmes, handling mistakes, and building operational systems.

### 4. People Building the Ecosystem

Human conversations with founders, mentors, programme leads, partners, and supporters. Focus on
their insight and motivation rather than ceremonial introductions.

### 5. The Larger Mission

Why African founders need more build infrastructure, what David believes must change, where Beere
Softwares fits, and what Origin40 could become after this cohort.

## The weekly content machine

### Capture

Record small pieces during the work instead of staging everything later:

- Morning: a 20-second statement of today's stakes
- During work: three to five short clips of calls, planning, screens, venue/team moments, or whiteboard
- After a decision: explain what changed and why
- End of day: what worked, what failed, and what happens next
- Founder/mentor clips: one specific question, 30–90 seconds, consent confirmed before recording

### Produce

One weekly story becomes:

- 1 YouTube vlog
- 4 vertical videos
- 3 LinkedIn posts
- 5–10 Story frames across the week
- 1 photo/carousel recap
- 1 email reflection when the list is ready

### Weekly episode structure

1. **Cold open:** the hardest or most emotional moment
2. **The goal:** what must happen this week
3. **The obstacles:** decisions, setbacks, and pressure
4. **The people:** founders, team, mentors, or partners in action
5. **The result:** what actually changed
6. **The next stake:** what could go wrong or must happen next week

## Growth loops

1. **People loop:** tag featured founders, mentors, and partners; provide each person with a clean
   clip they are proud to share.
2. **Series loop:** number episodes and end every post with the next unresolved stake.
3. **Conversation loop:** ask specific questions (“What would you cut from this MVP?”), then use the
   best responses in the next post.
4. **Proof loop:** every programme milestone creates evidence; evidence creates content; content
   attracts future founders, mentors, partners, and opportunities.
5. **Owned-audience loop:** move the most interested followers to an email list or community once
   the signup destination is ready.

## Production rules

- Phone camera is enough; prioritise clear audio and honest scenes.
- Capture horizontally for YouTube when possible and a second vertical clip for short-form.
- Record in 10–30 second clips. Hold each shot still before moving.
- Always collect clean room tone and a few establishing shots.
- Maintain a shared folder by date: `YYYY-MM-DD / A-roll / B-roll / photos / releases`.
- Back up footage daily.
- Add burned-in captions to every short video.
- The first two seconds must contain the tension, result, or question.

## Consent and trust rules

- Confirm the programme's media consent covers personal-channel publication; programme consent and
  David's personal use should not be assumed to be identical.
- Ask before filming private calls, screens, applications, feedback, or emotional moments.
- Never show phone numbers, email addresses, financial data, application scores, private Discord
  messages, access credentials, or unreleased products without permission.
- Let a founder review sensitive transformation stories before publication.
- Do not film a setback merely because it is dramatic.
- Content is subordinate to the founder experience and programme delivery.
