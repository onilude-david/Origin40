# Origin40 — Launch Campaign Plan

**Campaign line:** *4 Weeks. 40 Founders. Real Products.*
**Goal:** 300+ quality applications → select 40 founders; recruit mentors, partners, sponsors;
fill Demo Day; tell the story afterwards.

Track every post in Sheets tab `10 · Content Calendar`; metrics roll up in `10b · Marketing Dashboard`.

## The 10 phases (with goals, channels, KPI)

| # | Phase | Goal | Primary channels | KPI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pre-launch Awareness** | Build curiosity; grow audience | IG, LinkedIn, X, WhatsApp status | Followers, reach |
| 2 | **Application Announcement** | Open applications loudly | All + email + press | Landing-page visits |
| 3 | **Founder Recruitment** | Drive qualified applies | IG, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, communities | Applications started |
| 4 | **Mentor Recruitment** | Confirm mentors across 8 roles | LinkedIn, email, warm intros | Mentors confirmed |
| 5 | **Partner/Sponsor Campaign** | Close sponsors across tiers | Email, LinkedIn, decks | ₦/$ committed |
| 6 | **Deadline Push** | Urgency before close | All + WhatsApp broadcast | Apply conversion |
| 7 | **Selected Founders Announcement** | Celebrate the 40 | IG carousel, LinkedIn, press | Reach, applause |
| 8 | **Weekly Updates** | Show the build (FOMO) | IG stories, LinkedIn, X | Engagement |
| 9 | **Demo Day Campaign** | Fill the room + investors | Email, LinkedIn, press, partners | RSVPs, investors |
| 10 | **Post-program Storytelling** | Prove impact; set up next cohort | All + impact report | Shares, leads |

## Timeline (relative to applications opening = Day 0)

- **Day −21 → −8:** Phase 1 (awareness). Teasers, countdown, founder/mentor "why".
- **Day −7 → 0:** Phase 2 launch week. Big announcement, application link live.
- **Day 0 → +14:** Phase 3 + 4 + 5 in parallel (founders, mentors, sponsors).
- **Final 72 hours before close:** Phase 6 deadline push (daily WhatsApp + countdown).
- **Selection week:** Phase 7 — announce the 40.
- **Weeks 1–4 of program:** Phase 8 weekly updates.
- **Demo Day −14 → 0:** Phase 9.
- **Demo Day +1 → +30:** Phase 10 storytelling + impact report.

## Content pillars (rotate across all phases)

1. **Why Origin40** — the mission; "Where Africa's Next Tech Founders Build"; the David Oke Opeyemi legacy.
2. **For founders** — what you get (build, mentors, Demo Day, opportunities), who should apply.
3. **Proof & people** — mentors, partners, judges, past wins, founder stories.
4. **The build** — behind-the-scenes, milestones, real products taking shape.
5. **Opportunity** — Demo Day, investors, prizes, what's next after the program.

## Weekly cadence (minimum)

- 4–5 feed posts/week · daily stories · 2 LinkedIn posts/week · 2 X threads/week ·
  1 WhatsApp broadcast/week (more during deadline push) · 1 email/week to the list.

## Roles (see `program-management/operations-manual.md`)

Marketing Lead owns the calendar; each post has an Owner and Status in tab 10.
Keep design in a simple template kit (post / story / carousel / quote) — see Brand checklist in tab 22.
