# David Onilude — X Content Calendar

**Window:** Monday, July 13, 2026 to Wednesday, August 12, 2026  
**Primary account:** David Onilude / `@onilude_david`  
**Campaign arc:** Building Origin40 in public  
**Working fact base:** 20 selected founders, 4-week online programme, Lagos showcase on August 3, 2026

## Bottom line

X should become David's live operator journal.

The goal is not to sound like a programme announcement page. The goal is to sound like a real
builder in Nigeria showing the work, the pressure, the lessons, the people, and the judgment behind
building Origin40, Beere Softwares, and founder-support systems in public.

## Voice rules

Write like this:

- "I noticed something today..."
- "This part is harder than people think..."
- "A founder said something that stayed with me..."
- "The work is not glamorous, but this is where things are built."
- "Building in Nigeria will humble you."

Avoid this:

- "I am thrilled to announce..."
- "We are empowering the ecosystem..."
- "Unlocking potential..."
- "Driving transformation..."
- "Leveraging innovation..."

## Daily operating rhythm

Minimum daily output:

1. **Morning post:** what today is about
2. **Midday post:** founder/operator lesson
3. **Evening post:** reflection, observation, or short story
4. **Replies:** 10 thoughtful replies under founders, builders, investors, operators, ecosystem people

Strong mode:

- 4-5 posts per day
- 15-20 replies
- 1 quote-tweet with David's own view
- 1 photo/video when there is a real moment

## Recurring formats

Use these repeatedly so people start recognising David's voice.

### Operator Log

Short post from inside the work.

Example:

> Operator Log: Day 1 of Origin40.  
>  
> Founders don't just need motivation.  
> They need structure, pressure, feedback, and people who will help them see clearly.

### Founder Note

Practical founder lesson.

Example:

> Founder note:  
>  
> If the problem is cloudy, the product will suffer.  
> Clarity is not packaging. Clarity is part of the product.

### My week in 4 pictures

Community-native post inspired by high-performing Nigerian builder accounts.

Example:

> My week in 4 pictures.  
>  
> Origin40 mode. Founders, calls, planning, and plenty follow-up.  
>  
> You?

### Building in Nigeria

Local, human, sharp.

Example:

> Building in Nigeria will humble you.  
>  
> The idea is one thing. Power is another. Trust is another. Distribution is another.  
>  
> Still, people are building.

Every day, David should engage with:

- 3 early-stage founders
- 2 Nigerian/African tech operators
- 2 investors, accelerators, or ecosystem people
- 2 software/product builders
- 1 media/community person

Reply style:

- Add a real opinion
- Ask a useful question
- Share one field observation
- Avoid "Great work" replies unless adding something specific

## Metrics to track weekly

Track every Sunday:

- Follower growth
- Best post by impressions
- Best post by replies
- Best post by profile visits
- Number of inbound DMs/opportunities
- Number of useful founder/partner relationships started
- Content formats that felt most natural to write

## Safety and consent rules

- Do not post founder names, pitches, screenshots, vulnerable moments, business details, or internal
  feedback without consent.
- Do not turn founders into content props.
- When in doubt, anonymise the lesson and protect the person.
- Show the work, but do not expose private work.
