## 1. Partner Value Proposition

**Back Africa's next generation of builders.** Origin40 takes 40 vetted early-stage African tech
founders from idea to real MVP to Demo Day in 4 weeks. Partnering gives your brand:
- **Visibility** to an engaged founder + ecosystem audience across the whole campaign.
- **Access** to a curated pipeline of 40 builders — for hiring, pilots, deal flow, or community.
- **Positioning** as a genuine supporter of African innovation (not just a logo).
- **Demo Day presence** in front of founders, mentors, media, and investors.

Powered by Beere Softwares · Partner: Equip Africa Initiative · Supported by Dooke Innovation Center & Anfani.

## 5. Partner Outreach & Follow-ups
*(Full email + follow-up sequence in `marketing/outreach-messages.md`.)*

**Pipeline discipline (tab 14):** every prospect has an Owner, a Stage, a Value, a Last Touch date,
and a Next Action. "Days Since Touch" auto-computes — don't let a warm lead go cold. Stages:
Identified → Contacted → Meeting Booked → Proposal Sent → Negotiating → Committed → (or Declined).

**Follow-up cadence:** Day 0 send · Day +4 nudge · Day +7 value-add · Day +12 final. Then park or close.
Committed sponsors move to fulfilment: agreement → asset specs → logo live → Demo Day logistics → thank-you.
