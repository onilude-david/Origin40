## Operating Notes

- Tuesday and Thursday remain independent execution days during the three online build weeks.
- August 3 is an in-person Founder Showcase, Networking & Media Day in Lagos and the official
  programme closing date. Founders are not being scored or judged through formal pitches.
- Discord carries async check-ins, evidence uploads, support requests, and deadline reminders.
- Every live mentor or guest session must end with a founder artifact or documented decision.
- Pending mentors and invited guest speakers are excluded until the programme owner confirms them.
- The cPanel Calendar contains the detailed timed blocks and generates CSV and ICS downloads.
