## Verification Calls

Run 10-minute calls for the ten conditional candidates and any recommended candidate carrying a
material risk. Ask only the questions needed to make the decision:

1. What exact artifact will you demonstrate on August 3?
2. Who will personally build it during the programme?
3. Can you attend every required online session and commit 10-15 hours weekly?
4. Do you have reliable laptop and internet access for the full programme?
5. Which three real users or customers will test the product?
6. What regulated, sensitive-data, or safety boundary applies to your product?
