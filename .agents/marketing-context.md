**Last updated:** July 9, 2026  
**Status:** V1 working context

## Bottom line

David Onilude's personal public story is the operator's view of building Origin40: the decisions,
pressure, people, mistakes, systems, and outcomes behind helping early-stage African founders move
from ideas to real products.

- 🟢 **Verified:** Origin40 is a build-focused founder incubator powered by Beere Softwares.
- 🟢 **Verified:** The 2026 programme runs online from July 13–31, followed by an in-person Founder
  Showcase, Networking & Media Day in Lagos on August 3.
- 🟢 **Verified:** Twenty founders were selected from the current application cycle.
- 🟢 **Verified:** The programme includes product, technology, design, growth, legal, operations,
  mentoring, founder storytelling, and media preparation.
- 🟢 **Verified:** Origin40 was launched in honour of David Oke Opeyemi at 40.

## Brand voice

- Grounded
- Ambitious
- Candid
- Practical
- Warm

### Do

- Speak as a builder still learning
- Use concrete scenes, numbers, decisions, and outcomes
- Give credit generously to founders, team members, mentors, and partners
- Show uncertainty without manufacturing drama
- Teach from fresh experience

### Do not

- Perform fake certainty or motivational-guru energy
- turn founders into props for David's personal brand
- exaggerate impact, traction, or programme results
- publish private applications, internal scores, sensitive messages, or vulnerable moments without
  explicit consent
- let content production disrupt founder delivery
