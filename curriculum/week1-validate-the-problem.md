# Week 1 — Validate the Problem

**Outcome:** a sharp, evidenced problem and a clearly defined target customer.
**Milestone:** Problem-Validation Brief (submitted Friday).

### Monday Guest Mentor Masterclass — Founder-Market Fit & Customer Discovery

**Duration:** 1-2 hours.
**Ideal mentor:** founder, product leader, investor, operator, or growth lead who has built from
customer insight in an African/emerging-market context.

**Class flow:**
1. Why most startups fail before they build: weak problem selection.
2. Founder-market fit: why this founder should solve this problem now.
3. Customer discovery teardown: good vs. bad interview questions.
4. Live work: each founder rewrites their problem statement and interview script.
5. Mentor picks 3-5 founders for follow-up office hours.

**Build consequence:** no founder leaves Monday without a clear person to interview, a non-leading
interview script, and a Friday evidence target.

### Friday Demo Gate — Problem Evidence

Each founder has 3 minutes to present:

1. The problem statement.
2. The target customer.
3. Evidence from interviews.
4. The strongest quote or pattern.
5. The smallest product direction for Week 2.

Mentors score the brief immediately and flag founders who are too broad, too solution-led, or
missing evidence.

## Module 1.1 — Find a Problem Worth Building

### Lesson 1.1.1 — From idea to problem
**Description:** Founders fall in love with solutions; investors and users care about problems.
This lesson reframes your idea as a specific, painful, frequent problem for a specific person.
**Learning objectives:**
- State your problem in one sentence: *"[Who] struggles to [do what] because [why]."*
- Distinguish a *real* problem (painful, frequent, urgent) from a *nice-to-have*.
- Identify who has this problem most acutely (your beachhead user).
**Assignment:** Write your problem statement + 3 reasons you believe it's real (with any evidence).
**Submission:** Google Doc link + 2-sentence summary, via LMS upload.
**Worksheet — Problem Statement Canvas:**
| Field | Your answer |
|---|---|
| Who (specific person/role) | |
| What they're trying to do | |
| Why it's hard today | |
| How often it happens | |
| How they cope now (workaround) | |
| One-sentence problem | |
**Reflection:** Whose problem is this *really* — yours, or a customer's? What would make you wrong?
**Mentor rubric (/10):** Specificity of problem (4) · Evidence it's real (3) · Clarity of target person (3).

### Lesson 1.1.2 — Know your customer
**Description:** Define the exact person you're building for so every later decision has an owner.
**Learning objectives:** Build a one-page customer persona; size who is reachable in 4 weeks.
**Assignment:** Complete the persona for your beachhead customer.
**Submission:** Add the persona to your brief doc.
**Worksheet — Customer Persona:** Name/role · context · goals · frustrations · where to find them ·
what they'd pay/switch for · 3 places you can reach 5 of them this week.
**Reflection:** Can you reach 5 of these people in the next 7 days? If not, narrow the segment.
**Mentor rubric (/10):** Realistic/reachable segment (4) · Depth of insight (3) · Specificity (3).

## Module 1.2 — Talk to Users (Validation)

### Lesson 1.2.1 — The customer interview
**Description:** How to interview users without leading them — the discipline behind *The Mom Test*.
**Learning objectives:** Run a non-leading interview; ask about past behaviour, not future opinions.
**Assignment:** Draft your interview script (5–7 questions about their life, not your idea).
**Submission:** Script in brief doc.
**Worksheet — Interview Script starter:**
1. Walk me through the last time you faced [problem].
2. What did you do about it? What did that cost you (time/money/stress)?
3. What's the hardest part? Why is *that* hard?
4. What have you tried? What did you dislike about it?
5. (Never pitch.) Who else feels this strongly?
**Reflection:** Which of your questions secretly fish for a "yes"? Rewrite them to be neutral.
**Mentor rubric (/10):** Non-leading questions (4) · Focus on real past behaviour (3) · Usefulness (3).

### Lesson 1.2.2 — Run 5 interviews
**Description:** Evidence beats opinion. Five honest conversations will reshape your problem.
**Learning objectives:** Conduct and synthesise 5 interviews; extract patterns.
**Assignment:** Complete **5 user interviews** and log them.
**Submission:** Interview Log (Google Sheet) link + top 3 insights.
**Worksheet — Interview Log:** columns = Person · Date · Has the problem? (Y/N) · How they cope ·
Most painful quote · Willing to try a solution? (Y/N).
**Reflection:** What surprised you? What belief did you have to drop?
**Mentor rubric (/10):** 5 interviews completed (4) · Quality of insight (3) · Honesty about being wrong (3).

## Module 1.3 — Market & Brief

### Lesson 1.3.1 — Market relevance & opportunity
**Description:** Right-size the opportunity: who else has this problem, and is the market growing?
**Learning objectives:** Estimate a simple TAM/SAM/SOM; articulate why now.
**Assignment:** One-paragraph market note + a "why now" sentence.
**Submission:** Add to brief.
**Worksheet — Market snapshot:** Total who have the problem · realistically reachable · winnable in 12 months · 1 trend making this urgent now.
**Reflection:** If this works, is it big enough to matter? If not, can you widen it without losing focus?
**Mentor rubric (/10):** Sensible sizing (4) · Credible "why now" (3) · Focus (3).

### Lesson 1.3.2 — Submit the Problem-Validation Brief (Week 1 Milestone)
**Description:** Package the week into a one-page brief — the foundation for everything you build.
**Learning objectives:** Synthesise problem, customer, evidence, market into a crisp brief.
**Assignment — MILESTONE:** Submit the **Problem-Validation Brief**.
**Submission:** Single Google Doc, view access, + 3-sentence summary, via LMS.
**Worksheet — Problem-Validation Brief (template):**
1. Problem statement (1 sentence)
2. Target customer (persona summary)
3. Evidence (5 interviews — top 3 insights + 1 killer quote)
4. How they cope today
5. Market snapshot + why now
6. The smallest thing you could build to help (1 sentence → feeds Week 2)
**Reflection:** On a scale of 1–10, how sure are you this problem is real? What would move it +2?
**Mentor rubric — Week 1 Milestone (/10):** Problem clarity (2) · Evidence/interviews (3) ·
Customer definition (2) · Market sense (1) · Readiness to build (2).
