# Origin40 Founder Build Schedule

**Program model:** 4 weeks. 40 founders. Real products.
**Core rule:** every founder must build, test, improve, and pitch something real.

This schedule is the standard operating rhythm for the incubator. It is designed for a tight,
high-accountability founder program with two on-site days each week:

- **Monday on-site:** alignment, guest mentor masterclass, sprint planning, build kickoff.
- **Friday on-site:** demo gate, submission review, mentor feedback, next sprint decision.
- **Tuesday to Thursday remote/build days:** execution, customer work, office hours, async review.

The curriculum is not a lecture series. The classes exist to improve the build. Every session must
end with a concrete founder action, a visible artifact, or a decision that moves the product forward.

---

## Weekly Time Standard

| Day | Mode | Standard schedule | Required output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | On-site | 30m founder standup · 90-120m guest mentor masterclass · 60m workshop · 2-3h supervised build sprint · 30m commitments | Weekly sprint plan + first artifact |
| Tuesday | Remote/build | Founder execution · customer work · async check-in · mentor office hours | Evidence of build/customer progress |
| Wednesday | Remote/build | Build block · technical/product clinic · accountability check | Midweek progress update |
| Thursday | Remote/build | Polish, testing, documentation, submission prep | Draft milestone submission |
| Friday | On-site | 45m standup · 2-3h build/demo sprint · 90m demo gate · 60m mentor review · 30m next actions | Scored weekly milestone |

## Guest Masterclass Standard

Each special guest faculty member takes **one 1-2 hour applied class**. They are not ongoing mentors
and are not expected to carry founder capacity after the class. They do not simply speak; they teach
from experience, break down examples, and push founders into action.

Every guest masterclass session must include:

1. **Founder-relevant insight:** what founders must understand this week.
2. **Real example:** teardown of a startup, product, pitch, campaign, or failure.
3. **Tactical framework:** a repeatable tool founders can use immediately.
4. **Live application:** founders apply the framework to their own venture during the session.
5. **Office-hour handoff:** guest identifies 3-5 founders who need follow-up support from the
   program team, facilitators, or regular mentors.

## Full Founder Stack Coverage

Each week has one primary build goal and supporting founder disciplines. The specialist tracks are
not separate courses; they are applied clinics that improve the venture being built.

| Week | Build goal | Business | Law/compliance | Finance | Tech/product |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Validate a real problem | Customer segment, market relevance, founder-market fit | Consent for interviews, ethical data capture, privacy basics | Market size logic, willingness-to-pay signals | Discovery tools, research repository, evidence log |
| Week 2 | Build MVP/prototype v1 | MVP scope, value proposition, channel assumptions | IP ownership, collaborator agreements, product disclaimers | Cost to build, pricing hypothesis, unit cost assumptions | Architecture, no-code/code/AI stack, analytics setup |
| Week 3 | Test and prove demand | Traction metric, sales discovery, growth loop | User test consent, data handling, terms/privacy draft | Traction economics, early revenue/pilot signal | Testing, analytics, iteration, reliability |
| Week 4 | Pitch and launch | Business model, partnerships, go-to-market | Founder readiness, confidentiality, customer terms, legal checklist | Budget, runway, funding ask, use of funds | Demo readiness, technical roadmap, post-program build plan |

## Specialist Clinic Slots

Use these as rotating 45-60 minute clinics on Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday, depending on mentor
availability:

- **Business clinic:** positioning, business model, partnerships, go-to-market.
- **Legal clinic:** founder agreements, IP, data/privacy, terms, consent, compliance basics.
- **Finance clinic:** pricing, cost model, runway, funding ask, sponsor/funder readiness.
- **Tech clinic:** product architecture, stack decisions, AI/no-code/code build support, analytics.
- **Growth clinic:** acquisition, community, sales scripts, experiments, traction evidence.
- **Pitch clinic:** deck narrative, demo flow, Q&A, investor/funder readiness.

## Founder Accountability System

Every founder must maintain four living artifacts:

- **Build Log:** what was built, blocked, changed, and shipped each day.
- **Customer Evidence Log:** interviews, tests, quotes, feedback, usage, signups, pilots.
- **Decision Log:** what changed in the product/market/pitch and why.
- **Demo Link:** the most current viewable version of the product/prototype.

The program team checks these artifacts on Mondays and Fridays. Missing artifacts affect completion.

## Friday Demo Gate

Friday is not a normal class day. It is a gate.

Each founder/team must show:

1. What changed since Monday.
2. The current product/prototype/demo.
3. Evidence gathered from users or customers.
4. The biggest blocker.
5. The next action before the following Monday.

Scoring happens in `07 · Submissions`, mentor feedback goes into `13 · Mentor Feedback`, and risk
flags go into `08 · Support Desk` or `05 · Progress Tracker`.

## Program Standards

- No passive attendance. Founders must produce visible work every week.
- No hidden progress. Work must be linked, demoed, or documented.
- No vanity validation. Evidence must come from real users, customers, usage, signups, pilots, or sales conversations.
- No overbuilding. Each week has a narrow milestone and a Friday gate.
- No Friday surprise. Founders must surface blockers by Wednesday.

## Completion Bar

A founder is considered program-complete only if they meet all of these:

- At least 70% attendance.
- At least 75% overall progress score.
- All four weekly milestones submitted.
- A visible product/prototype/demo exists.
- At least 5 customer interviews and 5 user/product tests completed.
- Final pitch deck, demo link, and 30-day roadmap submitted.
