**Outcome:** a clear business model and a confident 3-minute Demo Day pitch.
**Milestone:** Pitch Deck + live demo (Demo Day: 3-min pitch + 2-min Q&A).

### Monday Guest Mentor Masterclass — Pitch, Fundraising Readiness & Storytelling

**Duration:** 1-2 hours.
**Ideal mentor:** investor, accelerator lead, founder who has raised or sold, pitch coach, ecosystem
builder, or corporate innovation partner.

**Class flow:**
1. What judges, investors, and partners actually listen for.
2. The evidence-led pitch: problem, customer, product, traction, model, ask.
3. Common founder pitch mistakes: too much story, weak ask, no proof, unclear demo.
4. Live work: each founder rewrites their hook, traction slide, and ask.
5. Hot seat: 3-5 founders pitch for live critique.

**Build consequence:** every founder leaves Monday with a tighter pitch story, a draft ask, and a
clear Demo Day readiness checklist.

### Friday Demo Gate — Demo Day Standard

Each founder must deliver:

1. 3-minute pitch.
2. Product/prototype demo or backup video.
3. 2-minute Q&A.
4. Final ask.
5. 30-day post-program roadmap.

Mentors score founders against the Demo Day rubric and issue final fixes before the public showcase.

## Module 4.1 — Business Model & Viability

### Lesson 4.1.1 — Lean Canvas & how you make money
**Description:** Connect problem, solution, customer, and revenue into one coherent model.
**Learning objectives:** Complete a Lean Canvas; state a credible revenue model and unit economics (rough).
**Assignment:** Completed Lean Canvas + one-line revenue model.
**Submission:** Lean Canvas link + summary.
**Worksheet — Lean Canvas (9 blocks):** Problem · Customer Segments · Unique Value Proposition ·
Solution · Channels · Revenue Streams · Cost Structure · Key Metrics · Unfair Advantage.
**Reflection:** Will someone actually pay (or give attention/data of real value)? Why are you sure?
**Mentor rubric (/10):** Coherent canvas (4) · Credible revenue (3) · Realistic costs (3).

### Lesson 4.1.2 — Traction-to-ask: what you need next
**Description:** Define exactly what would 10x this: funding, pilots, users, partners, or talent.
**Learning objectives:** Translate progress into a specific, fundable ask.
**Assignment:** Your "ask" slide content: what you need + what you'll do with it.
**Submission:** Add to deck.
**Worksheet — The Ask:** Milestone reached · next milestone · what's needed (₦/$, intros, pilots) ·
what the supporter gets.
**Reflection:** Is your ask specific and proportionate to your evidence? Vague asks get vague answers.
**Mentor rubric (/10):** Specific ask (4) · Tied to traction (3) · Realistic (3).

## Module 4.2 — Craft the Pitch

### Lesson 4.2.1 — The 10-slide pitch deck
**Description:** A Demo Day deck tells a story in 10 slides and 3 minutes.
**Learning objectives:** Build a tight, visual deck; one idea per slide.
**Assignment:** Draft the 10-slide deck.
**Submission:** Slides link (Google Slides/Canva/PDF) + summary.
**Worksheet — 10-slide structure:**
1. Hook / one-liner · 2. Problem · 3. Who has it (customer) · 4. Solution / product ·
5. Demo (screenshot) · 6. Traction (your Week 3 evidence) · 7. Market / why now ·
8. Business model · 9. Team + why you · 10. The ask + contact.
**Reflection:** If a judge remembered ONE slide, which should it be? Make it your strongest.
**Mentor rubric (/10):** Story flows (4) · One idea/slide (3) · Strong traction slide (3).

### Lesson 4.2.2 — The 3-minute pitch script & delivery
**Description:** Write and rehearse a spoken pitch that fits 3 minutes with room for a live demo.
**Learning objectives:** Write a tight script; deliver with confidence; handle Q&A.
**Assignment:** Pitch script + record a rehearsal (Loom/video).
**Submission:** Script + rehearsal video link.
**Worksheet — Pitch script timing:** 0:00 hook (15s) · 0:15 problem+customer (45s) ·
1:00 solution+live demo (60s) · 2:00 traction (30s) · 2:30 model+ask (30s).
**Q&A prep:** list the 8 hardest questions (market, money, competition, why you) + your answers.
**Reflection:** Where do you ramble? Cut 20% of your words — clarity wins pitches.
**Mentor rubric (/10):** Fits 3 min (3) · Clear & confident (3) · Strong demo (2) · Q&A readiness (2).

## Module 4.3 — Launch & Demo Day

### Lesson 4.3.1 — Launch checklist & first users
**Description:** Put the product somewhere real and invite your first users / waitlist.
**Learning objectives:** Make the product publicly accessible; line up a launch moment.
**Assignment:** Live/public link + a simple launch post (for social + WhatsApp).
**Submission:** Public link + launch post copy.
**Worksheet — Launch checklist:** public URL works on mobile · clear CTA · way to contact you ·
analytics/signup capture on · launch post scheduled.
**Reflection:** What's the one thing that must not break on launch day? Test it twice.
**Mentor rubric (/10):** Publicly usable (4) · Clear CTA (3) · Launch readiness (3).

### Lesson 4.3.2 — Demo Day (Final Milestone)
**Description:** Pitch to judges, mentors, partners, and investors. 3-min pitch + 2-min Q&A.
**Learning objectives:** Deliver a confident, evidence-led pitch and demo under time.
**Assignment — FINAL MILESTONE:** Deliver your **Demo Day pitch** + submit final deck and product link.
**Submission:** Final deck + live product + 1-paragraph "where we are / what's next", via LMS.
**Worksheet — Demo Day readiness:** deck final · demo tested on venue wifi + offline backup video ·
script rehearsed ×2 with a mentor · Q&A answers ready · one-pager printed for investors.
**Reflection:** What will you commit to building in the 30 days after Demo Day? (Feeds the follow-up tracker.)
**Mentor/Judge rubric — Demo Day (/80):** Problem clarity · Product progress · Market opportunity ·
Validation evidence · Business model · Founder strength · Pitch quality · Impact potential (10 each).
