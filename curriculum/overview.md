# Origin40 Curriculum — 4-Week Founder Build Sprint

**Build the Product. Launch the Venture.**

Copy-ready for WordPress LMS, but designed first as a **build-focused incubator curriculum**.
Each week = one sprint; each sprint has guest mentor input, supervised build time, customer work,
and a Friday demo gate. Mirror build status into Sheets tab `09 · Curriculum Map`.

## The arc

| Week | Theme | Founder outcome | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Validate the Problem** | A sharp, evidenced problem + target customer | Problem-Validation Brief |
| 2 | **Design & Build the MVP** | A working prototype / MVP of the smallest valuable slice | MVP/Prototype v1 |
| 3 | **Test & Validate** | Real user feedback + early traction signal | Validation Report |
| 4 | **Pitch & Launch** | Business model + a 3-minute Demo Day pitch | Pitch + Demo |

## Weekly operating rhythm (every week)

- **Monday on-site:** founder standup, 1-2 hour guest mentor masterclass, sprint planning,
  supervised build sprint, and weekly commitments.
- **Tuesday-Thursday remote/build:** execution, customer work, mentor office hours, async progress
  checks, and support desk escalation.
- **Friday on-site:** build sprint, demo gate, milestone submission, mentor review, and next actions.
- **Async all week:** WordPress LMS lessons, Discord cohort server, accountability pairs,
  build logs, customer evidence logs, and mentor feedback.

See `founder-build-schedule.md` for the full operating schedule.

## Guest masterclass structure

Special guest faculty run **one 1-2 hour applied class**. They are not ongoing mentors and should
not be assigned founder capacity. Every class must produce founder action: a sharper problem, a
better product decision, a tested workflow, a growth experiment, or a stronger pitch. Guest sessions
are placed on Monday to shape the build sprint, with follow-up handled by facilitators or regular
mentors.

| Week | Guest mentor focus | Build consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer discovery, problem selection, founder-market fit; Samuel Afolabi on building from local pain | Better interview scripts and sharper problem briefs |
| 2 | MVP architecture, product design, rapid prototyping; Gbemi Adunbarin on commerce, AI, and trust | A smaller, working core flow |
| 3 | User testing, traction, growth loops, sales discovery; Dr. Abiola Olamoyegun on security, risk, and credibility | Real feedback, traction evidence, and practical product-risk controls |
| 4 | Pitch, fundraising readiness, storytelling, Demo Day delivery; Prof. Carlos Azevedo on impact and venture story | A credible pitch, demo, ask, impact thesis, and 30-day plan |

## Founder competency stack

Origin40 touches every base a serious early-stage founder needs. The program is still build-first,
but the build is surrounded by the disciplines that make a startup investable, legal, fundable,
usable, and operationally credible.

| Track | What founders must learn | Where it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| Business | customer, market, business model, pricing, go-to-market, partnerships | Weeks 1, 3, 4 |
| Law & compliance | founder agreements, IP, data/privacy, customer terms, mentor/judge confidentiality | Weeks 1, 2, 4 |
| Finance | startup costs, unit economics, pricing, runway, funding ask, sponsor/funder readiness | Weeks 2, 4 |
| Tech & product | MVP scope, product architecture, AI/no-code/code choices, analytics, reliability | Weeks 2, 3 |
| Growth & sales | channels, messaging, traction experiments, user activation, pilots | Weeks 3, 4 |
| Operations | weekly execution, decision logs, support desk, risk management, team rhythm | All weeks |
| Pitch & storytelling | narrative, demo, evidence, Q&A, investor/funder readiness | Weeks 3, 4 |

## Specialist mentor and guest faculty bench

The program should recruit both one-off guest faculty and ongoing mentors across these categories:

- **Business mentor:** market strategy, business models, partnerships, founder-market fit.
- **Legal mentor:** company formation basics, founder agreements, IP, privacy, contracts, disclaimers.
- **Finance mentor:** budgeting, pricing, unit economics, runway, funding readiness.
- **Tech mentor:** MVP architecture, AI/no-code/code stack, technical feasibility, analytics.
- **Product/design mentor:** user flows, UX, prototyping, product quality.
- **Growth/sales mentor:** customer acquisition, experiments, content, community, sales discovery.
- **Pitch/funding mentor:** Demo Day storytelling, investor/funder lens, ask design.

Guest faculty should be scheduled according to the weekly sprint needs, not prestige alone. A great
guest session is one that changes what founders build or how they prove demand that same week.

## Founder build artifacts

Every founder maintains four living artifacts:

- **Build Log:** daily product progress, blockers, shipped changes.
- **Customer Evidence Log:** interviews, tests, quotes, usage, signups, pilots, sales conversations.
- **Decision Log:** product, customer, market, pricing, and pitch decisions with reasons.
- **Demo Link:** current live product, prototype, Figma, no-code build, GitHub repo, or Loom walkthrough.

## How to grade

Every assignment is scored **/10** on its rubric and logged in tab `07 · Submissions`.
Mentors add written feedback in tab `13 · Mentor Feedback`. Completion requires ≥75% overall
progress and ≥70% attendance (auto-tracked in tab `05 · Progress Tracker`).

## Standard submission format (applies to every assignment)

> **Submit via the WordPress LMS assignment upload.** Include: (1) a link (Google Doc / Figma /
> live URL / Loom video as specified), (2) set sharing to "anyone with the link can view", and
> (3) paste a 2–3 sentence summary of what you did and what you learned. Late work is flagged
> automatically; if you're blocked, open a Support Desk ticket before the deadline.
