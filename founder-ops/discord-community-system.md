# Origin40 — Discord Community System

Discord is the official day-to-day communication hub for Origin40.

Use Discord for cohort rhythm, announcements, build logs, mentor office hours, community support,
and Demo Day preparation. Use email for formal notices, WordPress LMS for curriculum delivery, the
Support Desk for trackable issues, and WhatsApp only for urgent broadcast/backup reminders.

## Server Roles

| Role | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Program Lead | full oversight and announcements |
| Operations Team | moderation, support, scheduling |
| Mentor | office hours, feedback, founder support |
| Guest Mentor | temporary access for masterclasses/clinics |
| Founder | cohort participant |
| Partner/Judge | limited event or Demo Day access |
| Alumni | post-program community |

## Core Channels

| Channel | Use |
|---|---|
| `#announcements` | official program announcements only |
| `#start-here` | rules, calendar, links, onboarding steps |
| `#weekly-briefings` | Monday sprint brief and Friday gate reminders |
| `#build-logs` | daily founder build updates |
| `#support-desk` | quick triage; trackable issues still go into the Support Desk |
| `#mentor-office-hours` | mentor availability and booking |
| `#resources` | useful links, templates, recordings |
| `#demo-showcase` | demos, pitch practice, public-ready assets |
| `#wins` | shipped work, interviews completed, traction signals |

## Weekly Channels

- `#week-1-problem`
- `#week-2-mvp`
- `#week-3-validation`
- `#week-4-pitch`

Archive week channels after each sprint, but keep them readable for accountability.

## Founder Posting Standard

Every founder posts a build update at least 3 times per week:

1. What I shipped or learned.
2. What is blocking me.
3. What I will do next.
4. Link to evidence, demo, interview notes, or build log.

## Moderation Rules

- Keep channels focused; move long side conversations into threads.
- No spam, harassment, hate speech, or unsolicited promotion.
- Do not share confidential founder information outside the server.
- Mentors advise and challenge; they do not build the product for founders.
- Anything requiring ownership, due date, or escalation must become a Support Desk ticket.

## Onboarding Checklist

- Founder joins Discord.
- Founder updates display name: `First Name — Startup`.
- Founder reads `#start-here`.
- Founder posts intro in the assigned intro thread.
- Founder finds weekly channel and first build-log instruction.
- Founder confirms LMS and dashboard access.
