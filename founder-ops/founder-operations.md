# Origin40 — Founder Operations System

Everything that keeps 40 founders moving, supported, and accountable. Operational data lives in
Sheets tabs `04`–`08`; this doc holds the policies, forms, and copy.

---

## 1. Founder Readiness Assessment (intake)

Scored at onboarding in tab `04 · Founder Readiness` (5 dimensions × /5 = /25). Category auto-assigns:

| Score | Category | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20–25 | **Ready to Build** | Clear problem, committed, coachable | Standard track |
| 14–19 | **Needs Refinement** | Promising but unfocused | Extra Week-1 mentor time |
| 8–13 | **Too Early** | Idea/commitment still forming | Pair with mentor; monitor closely |
| 0–7 | **Not a Fit** | Misaligned for this cohort | Redirect to community/next cohort |

Dimensions: Problem Understanding · Solution Clarity · Commitment · Time Availability · Coachability.

## 3. Progress Tracking & Completion Criteria

Tab `05 · Progress Tracker` computes **% Complete** = milestones (50%) + attendance (25%) + reviewed
submissions (25%).

**Completion criteria (auto-flagged "Completion Eligible? = Yes"):**
- ≥ 75% overall progress, **and**
- ≥ 70% attendance, **and**
- All 4 weekly milestones submitted (Reviewed status), **and**
- Pitched at Demo Day.

Completers receive a **Certificate of Completion** (see Demo Day system). Founders falling below
thresholds mid-program are auto-eligible to be flagged **At Risk** — trigger a check-in.

---

## 4. Attendance & Submissions

- **Attendance** (tab `06`): mark Present/Late/Absent/Excused each session; % auto-feeds progress.
- **Submissions** (tab `07`): one row per founder per assignment; mentors set Status + Score /10;
  late work auto-flags against `_Deadlines`.

---

## 5. Founder Support Desk

Tab `08 · Support Desk`. Any founder blocker becomes a ticket (auto ID + date, aging highlighted >3 days).

**Channels:** Discord cohort server (official day-to-day hub), Support Desk ticket (anything needing
tracking), mentor office hours (build/strategy), WhatsApp broadcast/backup.
**SLA:** acknowledge within 24h; High/Critical within 4h. Owner assigned per ticket.
**Categories:** Technical · Curriculum · Mentor · Personal/Logistics · Tooling/Access.

---

## 6. Weekly Operating Rhythm

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| **Mon** | Live build session (90 min) — teach core + set milestone |
| **Tue–Thu** | Build days — mentor office hours, support desk open |
| **Fri** | Submission deadline + mentor review/scoring |
| **Sat/Sun** | Optional async; team preps next week |

---

## 7. Communication Workflow

- **Announcements:** Discord announcements + Email, mirrored in LMS. WhatsApp may be used for urgent
  broadcast reminders only.
- **Day-to-day:** cohort Discord server.
- **Discord channel structure:** `#announcements`, `#start-here`, `#weekly-briefings`,
  `#build-logs`, `#support-desk`, `#mentor-office-hours`, `#resources`, `#demo-showcase`,
  plus week-specific channels (`#week-1-problem`, `#week-2-mvp`, `#week-3-validation`,
  `#week-4-pitch`).
- **Accountability:** founders paired ("build buddies") for weekly check-ins.
- **Escalation:** mentor → Mentor Coordinator → Program Lead. Support tickets for anything trackable.
- **Tone:** warm, direct, build-focused. Celebrate shipping, normalise being stuck.
