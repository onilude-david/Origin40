# Origin40 Guest Masterclass Faculty Lineup

This document maps the first confirmed/proposed special guest masterclass faculty into the 4-week
Origin40 build curriculum. These are not ongoing mentors. Each guest comes in for one focused
1-2 hour applied founder class: teach, teardown, framework, founder work block, hot seats, and
follow-up list.

**Operating rule:** guest masterclass faculty have `capacity = 0` in the cPanel because they are not
assigned to founders after the class. Any follow-up actions they identify are handed to the program
team, facilitators, or regular mentors.

## Session Briefs

### Samuel Afolabi - Founder Execution / Local Market Building

**Best week:** Week 1, Monday onsite.

**Class promise:** founders learn how to turn a real local pain into a focused product wedge
without getting lost in noise, market frustration, or overbuilding.

**Suggested agenda:**

1. The reality of building in Nigeria: what founders can and cannot control.
2. Broken market teardown: why local friction can become product opportunity.
3. Founder-market fit exercise: why this founder, why this problem, why now.
4. Live founder work block: rewrite the problem statement and customer segment.
5. Hot seats: 3-5 founders with unclear problem/customer assumptions.

**Founder artifact:** one-page Problem-Validation Brief update.

**Publishing caution:** confirm whether the intended Samuel Afolabi is the MyAgent founder or the
cybersecurity speaker/profile before publishing a public bio.

### Gbemi Adunbarin - E-commerce, AI Commerce, Product Trust

**Best week:** Week 2, Monday onsite or midweek product clinic.

**Class promise:** founders understand how to design a useful digital commerce/product workflow,
where AI helps, and where trust, payment, delivery, or support can break the product.

**Suggested agenda:**

1. What makes an online product or commerce workflow believable.
2. Where AI can improve discovery, support, personalization, or operations.
3. Trust teardown: why users abandon digital products.
4. Live founder work block: map the core flow from first visit to value.
5. Hot seats: commerce, marketplace, service, AI assistant, and community-product founders.

**Founder artifact:** MVP Core Flow + Trust Checklist.

### Dr. Abiola Olamoyegun - Cybersecurity, Governance, Risk

**Best week:** Week 3 midweek clinic or Friday review lens.

**Class promise:** founders learn practical security and risk basics that make early products more
credible, especially when handling users, payments, personal data, partners, or institutional buyers.

**Suggested agenda:**

1. Security is a founder responsibility, not only a technical function.
2. The minimum viable security checklist for early products.
3. Data/privacy and vendor risk basics.
4. Live founder work block: map product risks and controls.
5. Hot seats: founders handling sensitive data, payments, dashboards, enterprise users, or AI.

**Founder artifact:** Product Risk Map + Security Readiness Checklist.

### Prof. Carlos Azevedo - Social Innovation, Impact, Venture Purpose

**Best week:** Week 4, Monday onsite.

**Class promise:** founders learn how to connect venture model, stakeholder need, and measurable
impact without turning their pitch into vague social-good language.

**Suggested agenda:**

1. Social innovation as disciplined venture design.
2. Stakeholder map: who benefits, who pays, who adopts, who blocks.
3. Impact model: activities, outputs, outcomes, and evidence.
4. Live founder work block: write an impact thesis and 3 measurable outcomes.
5. Hot seats: impact, education, health, agriculture, civic, climate, and inclusion ventures.

**Founder artifact:** Impact Thesis + Stakeholder Map + Demo Day impact slide.
