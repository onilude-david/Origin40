# Origin40 Guest Masterclass Slot Picker

Origin40 guest faculty are invited for **one focused 1-2 hour masterclass**. This is not an ongoing
mentor commitment. Each session should teach from experience, include a practical founder work
block, and leave founders with one concrete artifact.

**Timezone:** West Africa Time (WAT), Lagos.  
**Program format:** 100% online. Monday and Friday are live online program days.

## How To Pick A Slot

Please reply with:

1. Your preferred session title or topic adjustment.
2. Your first-choice slot code.
3. Your second-choice slot code.
4. Whether you prefer 60, 90, or 120 minutes.
5. Your preferred platform, if any.

## Session Format

| Segment | Duration | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Opening context | 5-10 min | Why this topic matters for this week's build sprint |
| Experience / teardown | 15-25 min | Real example, pattern, failure, or case breakdown |
| Framework | 15-20 min | A tool founders can use immediately |
| Founder work block | 20-35 min | Founders apply the framework to their venture |
| Hot seats | 15-30 min | 3-5 founders receive live feedback |
| Handoff | 5 min | Guest flags founders needing follow-up |
