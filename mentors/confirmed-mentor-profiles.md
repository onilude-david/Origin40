## Damilola Obaro

**Status:** Confirmed  
**Track:** Legal/Operations  
**Suggested capacity:** 5 founders  
**Current role:** Senior Associate, Ukiri Lijadu  
**Best-fit support areas:** startup legal basics, company incorporation, corporate structuring,
intellectual property, data privacy, regulatory compliance, fundraising/legal readiness, employment
and labour basics, contracts, and founder risk management.

### Short Bio

Damilola ("Dami") Obaro is an enterprise lawyer with extensive experience advising businesses across
Nigeria's startup, corporate, and commercial landscape. His practice focuses on strategic and
commercially driven legal solutions that help businesses, from early-stage startups to established
enterprises, achieve their objectives, manage risk, and scale sustainably.

He has advised on a broad spectrum of transactions and business matters, including company
incorporation and market entry into Nigeria, corporate structuring, intellectual property
protection, data privacy and security, regulatory compliance, fundraising and financing
transactions, mergers and acquisitions, employment and labour matters, and real estate and property
acquisitions.

Dami is currently a Senior Associate at Ukiri Lijadu, where he advises local and international
clients on complex corporate, commercial, and transactional matters.

### Founder Support Lens

Dami should be positioned as a legal and operations mentor who helps founders become credible and
safe to work with. His value is not abstract legal theory; it is helping founders understand what
must be structured, protected, documented, or clarified before they scale, fundraise, hire,
partner, or handle user data.

### Recommended Founder Office-Hour Topics

| Topic | Founder output |
|---|---|
| Startup legal basics | Founder legal checklist |
| Incorporation and structure | Entity / structure decision notes |
| IP and product ownership | IP ownership checklist |
| Data privacy and security | Data handling risk checklist |
| Fundraising readiness | Legal readiness notes for investors |
| Employment and collaborators | Team/collaborator agreement checklist |
| Commercial contracts | Contract red flags and negotiation notes |

### Optional Masterclass Topic

If Dami is available for a one-off teaching session in addition to regular mentoring:

**Legal Foundations for Early Founders: Structure, IP, Data, and Fundraising Readiness**

**Founder artifact:** Founder Legal Readiness Checklist.

## Stanley Anigbogu

**Status:** Confirmed  
**Track:** Technical / Product  
**Suggested capacity:** 5 founders  
**Current role:** Founder/CEO, LightEd  
**Best-fit support areas:** climate-tech, clean energy, circular design, hardware/product
prototyping, STEM/STEAM education, storytelling, social impact, community innovation, and founder
resilience.

### Short Bio

Stanley Anigbogu is a Nigerian creative technologist, innovator, storyteller, and social
entrepreneur. He is the founder/CEO of LightEd, a social enterprise working to reduce energy poverty
and environmental waste by transforming plastic and electronic waste into renewable energy
solutions.

His work sits at the intersection of clean energy, circular design, climate action, community
innovation, and education. Public profiles describe him as an internationally recognized innovator
and filmmaker, with work focused on providing clean, affordable energy access to underserved
communities while using technology and storytelling to drive social change.

### Founder Support Lens

Stanley should be positioned as a build-focused technical/product mentor for founders working on
physical products, climate solutions, social impact ventures, education/STEM products, hardware
prototypes, community infrastructure, or ventures that need a stronger innovation story.

His value for Origin40 is helping founders move from "big mission" to practical prototype, field
validation, community adoption, and a story that makes the product understandable.

### Recommended Founder Office-Hour Topics

| Topic | Founder output |
|---|---|
| Turning social problems into buildable products | Product wedge note |
| Prototype thinking for hardware / climate ventures | Prototype scope sheet |
| Circular design and sustainability | Sustainability model note |
| Community adoption | Stakeholder and adoption map |
| Storytelling for impact ventures | Founder story / demo narrative |
| Youth/STEM/education innovation | Learning experience map |
| Clean-energy and infrastructure products | Field validation checklist |

### Optional Masterclass Topic

If Stanley is available for a one-off teaching session in addition to regular mentoring:

**From Waste To Working Product: Building Climate And Impact Ventures That Communities Use**

**Founder artifact:** Impact Product Wedge + Prototype Validation Checklist.
