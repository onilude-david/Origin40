## 1b. Special Guest Masterclass Faculty

Special guest masterclass faculty are different from regular office-hour mentors. They deliver
**one focused 1-2 hour applied class** tied to the weekly build sprint, then exit. They are not
expected to review founder work weekly, carry founder capacity, or join mentor-founder matching.

| Track | Ideal guest | Class output |
|---|---|---|
| Business | founder, operator, investor, ecosystem builder | sharper customer, market, model, partnership, or GTM decision |
| Law | startup lawyer, IP/privacy/contracts specialist | founder legal checklist, consent language, IP/privacy awareness |
| Finance | CFO, finance operator, investor, fund manager | pricing hypothesis, budget, runway, funding ask, use-of-funds logic |
| Tech | CTO, senior engineer, no-code/AI builder | realistic MVP architecture, stack choice, analytics, product risk map |
| Product/UX | product manager, designer, researcher | user flow, prototype quality, usability improvements |
| Growth/Sales | growth lead, marketer, sales operator | channel test, sales script, traction experiment |
| Pitch/Funding | pitch coach, investor, accelerator lead | stronger story, demo, Q&A, ask, and Demo Day readiness |

**Required tracking fields:** guest name, track, session title, week, date, duration, founder output,
recommended follow-up founders, feedback score, media consent, and status.

**Standard session format:** teach → teardown → framework → founder work block → hot seats →
follow-up list.

**Capacity rule:** set guest masterclass capacity to `0` in the cPanel. Capacity only applies to
regular mentors who will follow founders after the class.

`guest-masterclass-research-and-outreach.md` for research notes, session topics, invitation emails,

## 3. Mentor Onboarding Guide

**Welcome, mentor.** Here's everything you need.

**What Origin40 is:** a build-focused 4-week incubator. Founders ship a real MVP, validate it, and
pitch at Demo Day. Your job is to *unblock and sharpen*, not to do the work for them.

**Your commitment as a regular mentor:**
- ~2–4 hours/week over 4 weeks (office hours + milestone feedback), fully flexible.
- Review your founders' weekly submissions and leave feedback (tab `13`, or the feedback form).
- Optional: judge or attend Demo Day.

**The 4-week arc:** Week 1 Problem → Week 2 MVP → Week 3 Validation → Week 4 Pitch & Launch.
Each week has a milestone scored on a rubric (see `curriculum/`).

**How to mentor well:**
- Ask more than you tell. Push for evidence and focus over polish.
- Protect the scope — founders over-build; help them cut.
- Be honest and kind. Specific feedback beats vague encouragement.
- Flag at-risk founders early to the Mentor Coordinator.

**Logistics:** your founders + contacts: [link]. Office-hours schedule: [link]. Cohort calendar: [link].
Questions → [coordinator].

## 5. Mentor–Founder Matching (tab `12`)

Match on: founder's biggest need × mentor role × availability/capacity.
Record Match Reason; tab auto-flags **OVER CAPACITY** if a mentor exceeds their declared capacity.
Aim for 1 primary mentor per founder + access to the wider mentor pool for specific topics.
