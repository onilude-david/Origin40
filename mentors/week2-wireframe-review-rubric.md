# Week 2 Wireframe Review Rubric

**For:** reviewing mentors and facilitators — Segun Ajanaku, Stanley Anigbogu, Kazeem Quadri,
David Onilude, and Build Lab facilitators.
**Used at:** Wednesday, July 22 UX critique and the Friday, July 24 MVP gate.
**Founder input:** `curriculum/week2-wireframe-worksheet.md`.

Score out of 10. This scores the **decision quality**, not the drawing quality. A photograph of a
paper sketch can score 10. A polished Figma file with unresolved scope scores 4.

---

## Scoring

| Criterion | Points | What full marks looks like |
|---|---:|---|
| Scope is cut | 3 | One user, one job, one flow, stated in a single sentence with no "and also". Every screen survives the cut test. |
| The flow reaches value | 3 | You can trace a continuous path from opening screen to the value moment with no missing steps and no dead ends. |
| Honesty about what is real | 2 | Manual steps declared without prompting, including the volume at which they break. |
| Buildable in the time left | 2 | Build path named, matches the founder's actual skills and tools, fallback named. |

**Pass mark for the Friday gate: 6/10.** Below 6, the founder does not fail the programme — they
get a rescope session before Monday, July 27, and re-present.

---

## Ask these six questions in this order

Do not skip ahead. Most problems surface in the first two.

1. **"Say the whole flow in one sentence."** Hesitation, or a sentence containing "and also",
   means the scope is not cut. Stop here and rescope — the rest of the review is wasted otherwise.
2. **"Which screen can you delete?"** Every founder has one. A founder who says "none" has not
   tested their own frames. Make them delete it in the room.
3. **"What is fake here?"** Then: **"at how many orders a day does that break?"** Reward the honest
   number. A founder who claims everything is automated is either wrong or building the wrong thing.
4. **"Show me what happens when it goes wrong."** Empty state, error, out-of-service. If all three
   are missing, cap the flow score at 1 of 3.
5. **"Who builds this, and by when?"** If the answer names a developer they have not hired, or a
   budget they do not have, the plan is fiction. Redirect to no-code or manual-ops the same day.
6. **"What did you cut that hurt?"** Nothing cut means nothing decided. This is the question that
   separates a scope sheet from a scope decision.

---

## Red flags — intervene the same session

| Flag | What it means | Intervention |
|---|---|---|
| More than eight screens | Scope not cut | Delete screens live, in the room, before they leave |
| Two equally weighted buttons on a frame | Founder has not decided what the screen is for | Make them pick the primary action |
| Placeholder text throughout | Product not thought through, only laid out | Send them to fill in real content before the gate |
| "The AI will handle that" | Deferred thinking, not a feature | Ask what the non-AI version is. Build that. |
| Login, settings, profile drawn before the core flow | Building the shell, not the product | Cut all three from v1 |
| Waiting on someone else to build | No build path | Move to no-code or manual ops today, not Monday |
| Marketplace, network, or platform language | Scope is a vision, not an MVP | Find the single transaction underneath it and build only that |

---

## What reviewers do not do

- **Do not draw the wireframe for the founder.** The wireframe is where they make the cut. Handing
  them ours removes the only part that matters, and they cannot defend it at the gate because they
  did not decide it.
- **Do not build, or arrange to build, a founder's product.** Origin40 provides spec review,
  mentorship, tooling guidance, and critique. Founders own the build. This applies to every founder
  equally, including those already generating revenue.
- **Do not soften the scope conversation.** Thirteen days remain to August 3. A founder who leaves a
  review feeling comfortable about an oversized scope has been failed, not supported.
- **Do not reward polish.** Visual fidelity at this stage usually signals avoidance of the scope
  decision.

---

## Recording the review

Log per founder: score out of 10, the one screen they cut, what is manual, the build path, the
fallback, and any rescope action with a date. Rescores below 6 go to the Monday, July 27 session.
