# Origin40 Guest Masterclass Research & Outreach Pack

This pack turns the first guest masterclass faculty list into research-backed session briefs and
ready-to-send outreach. These guests are **not ongoing mentors**. Each person is being invited for
one focused 1-2 hour applied masterclass, with any follow-up handed to facilitators or regular
mentors.

## Research Standard

- Use public sources only.
- Do not publish uncertain claims in public-facing copy.
- Do not mention private emails, personal addresses, or scraped contact details.
- If identity match is not fully confirmed, write the outreach around the known relationship or ask
  the person to confirm the best-fit topic.

## 1. Samuel Afolabi

### Verified / Public Signals

- Public LinkedIn results identify Samuel Afolabi as founder of MyAgent, building Africa's direct
  rental marketplace / trusted real estate marketplace.
- Recent public posts discuss building in Lagos/Nigeria, PropTech, trust deficits, verified data,
  rental density, and founder resilience.
- There are multiple public Samuel Afolabi profiles, including cybersecurity-related profiles, so
  confirm the intended person before publishing a bio.

### Why He Fits Origin40

Origin40 founders need to understand how to build from real local friction without becoming
overwhelmed by broken infrastructure, unstable markets, and investor uncertainty. Samuel's public
writing around Nigerian PropTech, market trust, supply density, and founder stamina maps directly
to Week 1: problem selection and founder-market fit.

### Recommended Category

**Founder execution / local market building**

### Masterclass Topic

**Building From Local Pain: Founder-Market Fit in Broken Markets**

### Class Promise

Founders will learn how to convert frustrating local market realities into a focused product wedge,
instead of building generic solutions for abstract problems.

### Suggested 90-Minute Flow

| Time | Segment | Output |
|---:|---|---|
| 0-10 min | Founder reality: building where systems are imperfect | Founders name the market friction they are building around |
| 10-25 min | MyAgent-style market wedge: trust, data, supply density | Founders identify their own trust/data/supply wedge |
| 25-45 min | Why broken markets are not excuses; they are product briefs | Rewritten problem statement |
| 45-65 min | Founder-market fit work block | Founder-market fit note |
| 65-85 min | Hot seats for 3-5 founders | Concrete next validation action |
| 85-90 min | Handoff to facilitators | Follow-up list |

### Founder Artifact

- painful problem
- affected customer
- broken-market friction
- evidence needed this week
